## EVAL-11-001 — Candidate recall and retrieval efficiency

evaluation · domain 11 · [EVAL-11-001 — Candidate recall and retrieval efficiency](../../evaluation/candidate-recall.md)

Measure whether candidate generation retains independently established true matches while reducing eligible comparisons. This evaluation separates retrieval completeness from conditional scoring recall and final entity quality, and compares complementary passes through deduplicated marginal yield. It requires an explicit pairspace, independently assembled reference matches, and a defensible sampling design. Budget limits, incomplete labels, population shifts, and entity dependence can invalidate apparently strong results; cost and segment diagnostics therefore accompany aggregate recall.

## EVAL-11-002 — Pairwise discrimination and probability calibration

evaluation · domain 11 · [EVAL-11-002 — Pairwise discrimination and probability calibration](../../evaluation/pairwise-calibration.md)

Evaluate pairwise ranking, threshold decisions, and the reliability of claimed match probabilities as separate properties. The protocol requires held-out labels, a declared candidate-conditioned or population estimand, and sampling weights where review selection changes prevalence. Precision–recall curves, proper probability scores, reliability diagrams, and cost diagnostics support comparison without treating arbitrary evidence scores as probabilities. Entity dependence, selective labels, changing retrieval, and downstream constraints limit interpretation; final entity quality needs its own evaluation.

## EVAL-11-003 — Final entity-resolution quality and error diagnostics

evaluation · domain 11 · [EVAL-11-003 — Final entity-resolution quality and error diagnostics](../../evaluation/entity-resolution-quality.md)

Assess the final identity output rather than assuming pairwise scores determine entity quality. Complete reference partitions support cluster-induced pair precision/recall and record-averaged B-cubed metrics; sampled fully resolved entities require design-aware estimation instead of naive benchmark slicing. Overmerge, undermerge, assignment, and catastrophic-error diagnostics connect measurements to application costs. Reference completeness, dependent records, output semantics, and oracle label support are essential prerequisites. The protocol offers complementary views, not one universally optimal metric.

## EVAL-11-004 — Retrieval blind-spot audit and missed-pair estimation

evaluation · domain 11 · [EVAL-11-004 — Retrieval blind-spot audit and missed-pair estimation](../../evaluation/retrieval-blind-spots.md)

Estimate how many true matches candidate retrieval misses, rather than only measuring recall over already-emitted candidates. The protocol attributes loss to eligibility rules, caps and routing, samples pairs from outside the candidate set with known nonzero inclusion probabilities, and reports a design-weighted missed-true-pair ratio with entity-cluster-aware uncertainty. It relates end-to-end accepted-pair recall to partition-induced pair measures, candidate graph recovery potential, and independent truth discovery versus oracle augmentation. Unknown evaluation inclusion or zero review probabilities leave the estimand nonidentifiable, which must be reported rather than estimated away.

## EVAL-11-005 — Quality estimation with incomplete reference evidence

evaluation · domain 11 · [EVAL-11-005 — Quality estimation with incomplete reference evidence](../../evaluation/incomplete-reference-quality.md)

Decide which accuracy claims incomplete reference evidence can support before computing a metric. Separate finite known-positive recovery, probability-sampled estimates, complete sampled entities, positive-unlabeled assumptions and proxy diagnostics. Specify the pair or entity population, output denominator, unknown outcomes, inclusion design and adjudication uncertainty. Conservative bounds and sensitivity analyses can be more defensible than a point estimate. Independent reference acquisition remains necessary when selection, completeness or label reliability cannot identify the intended quality measure.

## EVAL-11-007 — Complete-output execution cost and replay evaluation

evaluation · domain 11 · [EVAL-11-007 — Complete-output execution cost and replay evaluation](../../evaluation/execution-cost-replay.md)

Compare execution, reuse or incremental repair only after specifying output equivalence, completion and state/history boundaries. Measure cold build, warm reuse, update, recovery, peak resources and total retained cost on declared workloads. A faster incomplete run is not an equivalent optimization. Requires realized versions, task coverage and independent replay controls; quality changes need separate candidate and entity evidence, while synthetic stress results do not predict population accuracy or production capacity.
