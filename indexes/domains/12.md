## DEC-12-001 — Choose an experiment design that separates mechanisms from system effects

decision · domain 12 · [DEC-12-001 — Choose an experiment design that separates mechanisms from system effects](../../decisions/experiment-design.md)

Choose between fixed-candidate comparisons, stage replacements, ablations, factorial designs, and end-to-end evaluations according to the question an entity-resolution experiment must answer. The card distinguishes conditional component value from deployable system value, requires explicit identity, reference, split, and cost assumptions, and explains when controls conceal rather than remove interactions. A simple frozen baseline remains credible; uncertainty and behavioral guardrails determine whether to adopt, reject, or gather more evidence, without asserting unmeasured gains.

## SOL-12-001 — Controlled comparison with frozen baselines and independent evaluation

solution · domain 12 · [SOL-12-001 — Controlled comparison with frozen baselines and independent evaluation](../../solutions/experimentation/controlled-comparison.md)

Operationalize an entity-resolution comparison by freezing a credible baseline, naming the changed mechanism, controlling shared inputs, and reserving an independent final evaluation. The protocol covers semantic dependencies, admissible stage substitution, entity/time/source leakage, paired uncertainty, stochastic repetitions, and practical adoption rules. It separates diagnostic ablations from end-to-end claims and records negative or inconclusive results. It requires valid reference support and project-defined guardrails; reproducible execution alone does not establish population accuracy or useful improvement.
