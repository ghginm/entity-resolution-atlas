## DEC-14-001 — Operational monitoring, independent audits and response

decision · domain 14 · [DEC-14-001 — Operational monitoring, independent audits and response](../../decisions/monitoring-response.md)

Choose how ER operational signals, independent outcome audits and response authority work together as sources and versions change. Compare periodic audits, stage-profile alerts and combined monitoring rather than treating drift as measured accuracy loss. Requires eligible-population denominators, pinned reference regimes and assigned incident owners. Targeted review can discover failures but cannot alone estimate population quality; recalibration, retraining, retrieval repair and suspension answer different diagnoses.

## SOL-14-001 — Versioned stage monitoring with independent outcome audits

solution · domain 14 · [SOL-14-001 — Versioned stage monitoring with independent outcome audits](../../solutions/governance/stage-monitoring.md)

Attach stage- and source-conditioned telemetry to versioned ER runs and route anomalies through diagnosis, authorized containment and controlled validation. Keep representative outcome acquisition separate from targeted incident review. Requires stable denominators, missing-telemetry states and response ownership; score shift or graph growth cannot certify accuracy loss, and fewer alerts cannot certify improvement. Combine source onboarding, compatibility checks and delayed-review accounting with independent quality evidence.
