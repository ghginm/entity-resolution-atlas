## DEC-08-001 — Choosing the resolution output and membership policy

decision · domain 08 · [DEC-08-001 — Choosing the resolution output and membership policy](../../decisions/resolution-strategy.md)

Choose independent pair decisions, partial one-to-one assignment, accepted-link closure, or constrained entity membership from the task's identity and cardinality contract. Distinguish semantic transitivity from noisy observed links, genuine cannot-links from weak or absent evidence, and pair-level objectives from entity-level harm. The comparison identifies credible baselines, prerequisites, incompatible configurations, downstream master responsibilities, and controlled evaluations without assuming clustering is required or universally safer.

## SOL-08-001 — Thresholds, abstention, and review policies

solution · domain 08 · [SOL-08-001 — Thresholds, abstention, and review policies](../../solutions/decision-resolution/thresholds-abstention.md)

Convert pairwise scores into link, non-link, or deferred decisions using explicit losses, validated operating points, and review constraints. A probability-based cost threshold is justified only for calibrated estimates in the stated decision population with specified pairwise losses. Review is an action with cost, delay, and residual error, not a free confidence band. Semantic vetoes require genuine incompatibility. Assignment and clustering may need global resolution rather than independent thresholding.

## SOL-08-002 — Partial one-to-one assignment for bipartite linkage

solution · domain 08 · [SOL-08-002 — Partial one-to-one assignment for bipartite linkage](../../solutions/decision-resolution/one-to-one-assignment.md)

Resolve competing cross-source candidates through a maximum-utility partial bipartite matching with at most one match at both endpoints and explicit unmatched alternatives. The method requires defensible source cardinality, comparable utilities, and sufficient candidate coverage; it does not turn arbitrary scores into probabilities. A derived greedy counterexample, uncertainty handling, duplicate-related exclusions, and controlled comparisons explain when joint assignment is useful and how to hand off stable membership without making master-record or accuracy guarantees.

## SOL-08-003 — Connected components of accepted identity links

solution · domain 08 · [SOL-08-003 — Connected components of accepted identity links](../../solutions/decision-resolution/connected-components.md)

Form a partition by closing accepted identity links under graph connectivity, including isolated records. This transparent baseline has linear traversal cost on a fixed materialized graph, but does not assess the truth of implied pair memberships. A false bridge can contaminate entire components despite high accepted-edge precision. The card separates missing evidence from negatives, explains graph and lifecycle prerequisites, and compares closure with constrained membership using entity-level tail-risk and rollback tests.

## SOL-08-005 — Ordered first-match policies with pure predicates

solution · domain 08 · [SOL-08-005 — Ordered first-match policies with pure predicates](../../solutions/decision-resolution/ordered-rule-policies.md)

Compose ER dispositions through a deliberately ordered sequence of pure predicates and terminal rules. Keep hard admissibility separate from soft evidence, resolve source parameters before matching, and preserve unknown states, defaults, priority, and trace semantics. A small explicit conditional ladder is the credible baseline; an ordered registry adds named checks and versioned configuration, not automatic accuracy. Requires trusted constraint scope and compatible evidence, score, policy, and resolver contracts, with regression witnesses for overlap, boundaries, and cluster-wide guards.
