## DEC-10-001 — Ground-truth acquisition for learning and independent evaluation

decision · domain 10 · [DEC-10-001 — Ground-truth acquisition for learning and independent evaluation](../../decisions/ground-truth-acquisition.md)

Choose which records, pairs or entities to investigate, separating informative training acquisition from evidence supporting population quality claims. Compare probability and stratified review, entity-centric reference construction, targeted active review, and weak or silver labeling with human adjudication. The choice depends on the annotation unit, accessible frame, identity semantics, reference completeness and review costs. A hybrid reserves an independent evaluation pool while targeting the learning budget; neither random splitting nor probability weighting repairs unknown response mechanisms or absent support.

## SOL-10-001 — Independent entity-centric reference construction

solution · domain 10 · [SOL-10-001 — Independent entity-centric reference construction](../../solutions/ground-truth/independent-reference.md)

Build evaluation references that can expose identities missed by production retrieval rather than certifying only its candidates. Sample seed records or independently framed entities, search across a declared record universe, and adjudicate supported memberships with provenance and unresolved cases retained. Separate training, calibration and evaluation uses; audit inclusion probabilities, completeness and reviewer errors. External identifiers and stratified pair review are alternatives when their assumptions fit. Search cost, rare positives, length bias and hidden members limit population claims even after apparent saturation.

## SOL-10-002 — Probability-sampled review with separate evaluation and learning pools

solution · domain 10 · [SOL-10-002 — Probability-sampled review with separate evaluation and learning pools](../../solutions/ground-truth/probability-sampled-review.md)

Acquire auditable pair labels through a probability sample of a declared frame, with optional stratification for rare matches, source coverage and costly errors. Reserve independent evaluation labels and spend a separate targeted learning budget without confusing the two distributions. Log sampling units, strata, draw and inclusion probabilities, response dispositions and annotation costs. The design supports evaluation only where sampling support and label evidence exist; unresolved reviews, frame omissions and extreme weights limit claims. Entity search and estimator derivations remain with their canonical owners.

## SOL-10-003 — Targeted active review for learning and high-risk diagnosis

solution · domain 10 · [SOL-10-003 — Targeted active review for learning and high-risk diagnosis](../../solutions/ground-truth/targeted-active-review.md)

Allocate a learning or diagnostic review budget to uncertainty, disagreement, diversity, scarce-source evidence and consequential cluster decisions rather than treating every unlabeled pair alike. Acquire auditable labels, retain abstentions and revise the learner or error hypotheses through logged feedback. Compare against uniform acquisition on a separate independent test population under both label-count and actual-cost budgets. The approach needs adequate evidence access and a protected evaluation pool; heuristic query scores and selective label yield do not establish population accuracy or active-learning guarantees.

## SOL-10-004 — Weak supervision with auditable labeling functions

solution · domain 10 · [SOL-10-004 — Weak supervision with auditable labeling functions](../../solutions/ground-truth/weak-supervision.md)

Generate provisional pair-training labels from explicit rules, external resources and other noisy sources that can vote match, nonmatch or abstain. Diagnose coverage, conflicts and copied evidence before choosing simple voting or a dependency-aware label model. Independent adjudication remains necessary to distinguish rule imitation from identity discrimination and operational benefit. This approach requires defensible label semantics, source lineage and informative overlap; scarce sources, class imbalance and self-reinforcing errors can make confident silver labels misleading.
