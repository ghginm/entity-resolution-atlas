## DEC-07-001 — How should candidate pairs be scored?

decision · domain 07 · [DEC-07-001 — How should candidate pairs be scored?](../../decisions/scoring-strategy.md)

Compare expert-weighted additive evidence, Fellegi–Sunter likelihood ratios, and supervised logistic regression without treating them as a mandatory progression. Choose using identity semantics, candidate selection, evidence dependencies, label representativeness, interpretability, and downstream losses. Distinguish ranking scores from probabilities and scoring from decision policy. Reliable identifiers may justify simpler rules; limited labels do not remove statistical assumptions. Controlled comparisons must hold candidate generation and evaluation populations explicit.

## HYP-07-001 — Context-dependent attribute reliability without circular supervision

hypothesis · domain 07 · [HYP-07-001 — Context-dependent attribute reliability without circular supervision](../../research/hypotheses/contextual-reliability.md)

Test whether constrained source- and time-aware evidence weighting reduces held-out decision cost relative to a pooled scorer with the same candidates and comparisons. The proposal is untested: contextual likelihoods motivate it, but sparse source pairs, label bias, circular reliability estimates, and population shifts can defeat it. Independent adjudication, prespecified sharing controls, task-appropriate holdouts, and predeclared precision and compute guardrails are prerequisites. Context is a conditional evidence model, not a universal ranking of trustworthy sources.

## SOL-07-001 — Additive soft matching

solution · domain 07 · [SOL-07-001 — Additive soft matching](../../solutions/matching-scoring/additive-soft-matching.md)

Combine explicit comparison features through a baseline and signed evidence contributions rather than requiring every field to agree. Expert weights provide a transparent reference specification; likelihood-ratio estimation or supervised fitting can replace manual weights while retaining an additive structure. Feature semantics, candidate conditioning, and conflicting evidence require deliberate treatment. A raw additive score is neither necessarily a probability nor a calibrated confidence, and learned or nonlinear variants are alternatives rather than mandatory upgrades.

## SOL-07-002 — Fellegi–Sunter evidence weighting

solution · domain 07 · [SOL-07-002 — Fellegi–Sunter evidence weighting](../../solutions/matching-scoring/fellegi-sunter.md)

Compare the distribution of observed comparison evidence among true matches with its distribution among non-matches. The joint likelihood ratio is the theoretical foundation; additive field weights require conditional independence within both classes. State-specific probabilities may be estimated from labels or a constrained mixture model, but unlabeled estimation is not assumption-free. Candidate conditioning, prior odds, dependency, and estimation uncertainty determine whether likelihood-ratio scores can support credible posterior probabilities and operational decisions.

## SOL-07-003 — Logistic regression for pairwise matching

solution · domain 07 · [SOL-07-003 — Logistic regression for pairwise matching](../../solutions/matching-scoring/logistic-regression.md)

Learn an interpretable conditional log-odds function from labeled comparison evidence and map it through the logistic link. Predictor independence is not required, but correlated features can destabilize individual coefficients and a restricted feature basis can miss interactions. Representative labels, candidate-population alignment, leakage-safe assessment, and calibration checks are central prerequisites. Regularization, constrained fitting, and richer additive functions are optional variants, not an inevitable progression beyond simpler expert or likelihood-ratio scoring.

## SOL-07-004 — Pooling and source-conditioned evidence reliability

solution · domain 07 · [SOL-07-004 — Pooling and source-conditioned evidence reliability](../../solutions/matching-scoring/source-aware-reliability.md)

Compare pooled, source-specific and partially pooled evidence models when fields have different error processes across source pairs or periods. Separate contextual match prevalence from class-conditional evidence reliability. Requires observed provenance, compatible comparison semantics and independent evaluation support for the claimed contexts. Pooling can stabilize sparse estimates while concealing heterogeneity; separate models can expose it while overfitting tiny groups. Hierarchical shrinkage is a proposed compromise, not a verified ER improvement or a substitute for unseen-source validation.

## SOL-07-005 — Select nonlinear, embedding and language-model comparators

solution · domain 07 · [SOL-07-005 — Select nonlinear, embedding and language-model comparators](../../solutions/matching-scoring/learned-neural-comparators.md)

Compare additive nonlinear models, boosted trees, ranking objectives, embeddings and language-model pair comparators against interpretable logistic, likelihood-ratio and rule baselines. Requires an explicit output role, admissible data access and evaluation evidence suited to the claimed population. Richer models can represent interactions or textual variation unavailable to a small feature basis, but can also learn shortcuts, confuse relatedness with identity, increase cost and fail under shift. Complexity is an experimental choice, not a maturity ladder.
