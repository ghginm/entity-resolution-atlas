## Wave 5 conceptual routing checks

The seven initiating questions in the authorization map to existing owners through
selected sections of [task patterns](agent-task-patterns.md): no-label evaluation,
rule refactoring, graph contamination, learned-versus-explicit scoring, source
onboarding, blocking comparison and reproducible artifacts. No new taxonomy or
search service was added. The catalog's supporting-guide pointer is conditional
on the guide's existence and is regression-tested. Opening a task section does
not require loading every pattern, every domain or all related cards.

The [integration template](project-atlas-integration.md) defines a consumer-project
note, not canonical project facts. Baseline and alternatives remain independent
choices across topology/scoring/resolution/lifecycle. Real implementation facts,
permissions, outcome evidence and rollback scope cannot be supplied by Atlas
examples. Losing a candidate, changing pair calibration, altering global membership
and revising master values remain different error levels.

## Tooling and anti-bloat review

Three observed RED → GREEN regressions add duplicate-tag rejection, grouped
warning-only identical summary detection and optional task-guide catalog routing.
The complete documentation suite has 49 tests. Existing orphan, evaluation-route,
relation direction/cycle, source, immutable inventory and L0/L1 freshness checks
remain in force. No gate was weakened. Exact duplication is only a clue; weak
or excessive tags, semantic overlap, contradictions and unsupported claim strength
remain substantive-review responsibilities. New physical/monitoring owners fill
previous L2 gaps; lifecycle and governance use guides instead of duplicate cards.

Advisory lengths are retained because assumptions, counterexamples and evaluation
contracts materially constrain use. They do not certify completeness. Detailed
runtime/design variants should extend existing owners or justified L3, not inflate
inventory. Pair probability, cluster confidence, behavioral equivalence and identity
truth are not interchangeable.
