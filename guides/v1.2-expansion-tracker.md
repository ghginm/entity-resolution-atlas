## Design decisions and non-goals

1. Acquisition, estimation and experimentation have different owners (10, 11, 12). One new family must not absorb all three responsibilities.
2. A targeted research sample is not automatically a representative evaluation sample. Probability weights do not repair unknown nonresponse or zero support. An independent holdout does not guarantee representativeness.
3. Synthetic/metamorphic success supports a stated behavior contract, not real-world accuracy; smaller graph components and fewer contradictions are diagnostics only.
4. No numerical adoption thresholds or gains are universal Atlas facts. Project owners must predeclare practical margins, costs, privacy and review authorization.
5. Every later method should link a credible baseline and an evaluation/experiment capable of distinguishing mechanisms. Merge near-duplicate drafts into existing owners.
6. Baseline AU-v1 outcomes are read only as published aggregate findings. Do not open evaluator keys or solve frozen cases during this authoring wave; the new benchmark belongs to a separate version and session.
