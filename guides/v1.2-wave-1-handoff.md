### Scientific corrections that must survive later edits

1. Evaluation inclusion probabilities are not production retrieval probabilities. A frozen black-box candidate set can be audited independently; internals may be needed for stage attribution, not total missed-pair estimation.
2. Pair assertions/numerators/denominators must use the same semantic pairspace; complete-record B-cubed is a different target. Source 4's entity-balanced and with-replacement estimators are not the card's record-average/without-replacement formula.
3. Known foreign records do not automatically reveal the number of foreign identities. Complete and partially searched true entities support different claims.
4. HT totals can be design-unbiased while ratios are generally biased. Known first-order inclusion does not automatically provide a variance estimator, and unknown response/zero support is not repaired by weights.
5. Finite-population random-pair design variance differs from shared-entity transfer/model uncertainty. Do not impose endpoint bootstrap universally or pretend pairs are iid for every claim.
6. Zero discoveries are possible under a sound design. Fixed-size integer limits, sequential stopping and simultaneous/ratio confidence need their actual scope.
7. New-identity transfer needs entity separation; existing-identity continuation can legitimately use declared historical state with forward-time/as-of evidence and no future-label feedback.
8. Research acquisition, weak labels, synthetic properties and graph diagnostics do not independently certify population quality. Passing behavioral tests is not world accuracy; copied LF votes are not independent evidence.
