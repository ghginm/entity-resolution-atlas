## Rubric disposition after fixes

- **Mechanism, assumptions, failure modes, alternatives and composability — supported for the bounded questions.** Each method uses explicit information, a credible simpler comparator, a cost model and a falsifiable evaluation route.
- **Evaluation estimand/source scope — supported with explicit limitations.** Known, design-estimated, assumption-dependent and diagnostic evidence remain separate. HT totals versus ratios, response versus selection and actual design versus future-world dependence are not interchangeable.
- **Falsifiability — supported.** Fixed-pool and integrated contrasts distinguish different mechanisms; unknown-heavy or wide-interval results remain inconclusive. Synthetic/behavioral violations cannot certify identity accuracy.
- **Economy and responsibility boundaries — supported.** Acquisition owns selection/adjudication; evaluation owns estimands; experiments own controls. Existing four metric cards were deepened rather than replaced. The added L3 contains specialized derivations, not repeated scorer theory. No extra object system, architecture ID or service was introduced.
- **Empirical utility/ER improvement — not applicable.** No new agent cohort or ER experiment was executed. Two critiques and passing structural tests are not performance evidence.

## Advisory warnings and unresolved limits

Length warnings remain deliberately visible: sampling, weak supervision and controlled experiments need their assumptions/counterexamples close to their decisions. The new bodies remain bounded rather than splitting each sampling variant into a shallow extra card. Exact warnings and word counts are preserved in the [validation report](../validation/v1.2/wave-1-validation.json); no validator limit was weakened. AR-05's keyword-hint warning already existed in the baseline and does not assert missing scientific failure analysis.

No blocking critique remains in the author disposition. Residual limits are substantive: completeness and reviewer reliability still need project evidence; sparse positives/unknown response can leave population quality unidentifiable; PU and LF models need unverified project assumptions; advanced survey intervals, sequential tests and cryptographic guarantees remain unexpanded. No independent statistical coverage study or new measured agent-utility comparison was performed. Later architecture/MLOps, algorithms, lifecycle and retrieval work remains in the [tracker](v1.2-expansion-tracker.md), not retroactively claimed as Wave 1 coverage.
