# Wave 3 evidence scopes and limitations

## Verification boundary

The shared [ledger](source-ledger.json) retains stable IDs; [provenance](retrieval-provenance.json) pins received bytes and selected scopes. Full-text retrieval does not mean a full scientific review of every result. This wave adds references 29–40 and revisits selected prior sources. No numerical improvement is transferred to a new ER population and no local model, index, matcher or dataset experiment ran.

Content review was author-only; independent scientific review is not attested. Selected-source verification does not resolve that review-independence limitation.

## Retrieval failures and retention

Unrelated exploratory downloads and failed source paths were excluded from the ledger and citations. Only the identified, pinned sources support the reviewed scopes above; failed retrievals provide no evidence.

Sources 35 and 38 were received over HTTP; hashes record bytes, not transport authenticity. Source 39 is a primary-paper copy at a third-party mirror. The selected MMDS definitions, set-cardinality argument and banding expression were readable despite extraction warnings; no unreadable glyph-dependent theorem was imported. The repository stores metadata, hashes and original analytical prose, not full copyrighted source texts.

Unicode received pages identify version 18.0.0/revisions 58 and 41. These are version labels of received bytes, **not a claim to have verified the latest release**. The generative-model paper is pinned to arXiv v4 and uses historical models/prices; no current model recommendation follows.

## Deliberate scientific gaps

Specialist transliteration, hierarchical/shrinkage estimators, monotone-ER methods, learning-to-rank algorithms, learned blocking, ANN internals, edit-distance-specific q-gram bounds and relational joint inference require selected primary mechanisms and appropriate project evidence before deeper adoption. The new cards explain role, failure conditions, baseline and a distinguishing experiment where possible; they do not invent missing specialist guarantees. Source authority, privacy, identity continuity and acceptable error costs remain external-project facts.

Naming a candidate method does not establish a tested hypothesis. Existing frequency and contextual-reliability hypotheses remain untested comparisons. Bridge review/repair is a bounded extension of closure/constraint reasoning, not a new ER algorithm or a measured improvement.
