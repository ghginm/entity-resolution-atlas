## DEC-01-001 — Define identity before choosing resolution

decision · domain 01 · [DEC-01-001 — Define identity before choosing resolution](../../decisions/identity-semantics.md)

Define the real-world object, granularity, namespace and time scope that a correct identity assertion denotes. Distinguish source records from entities and strict same-as from related-to edges before choosing labels, cardinality constraints or output structures. Compare enduring identity, time-scoped state identity and relationship-only linkage. The choice needs domain-owner adjudication of identifier reuse and continuity; neither similar attributes nor a convenient clustering algorithm supplies those rules. Ambiguous cases can remain unresolved rather than becoming false equivalences.

## SOL-01-001 — Enduring identity with time-scoped evidence

solution · domain 01 · [SOL-01-001 — Enduring identity with time-scoped evidence](../../solutions/identity-semantics/temporal-identity.md)

Maintain enduring entity identity while representing changing attributes as versioned, time-scoped observations rather than overwriting history. Separate validity, source observation and ingestion times, and use an explicit domain continuity policy for migration, identifier reuse and organizational succession. Compare with snapshot or episode identity when longitudinal continuity is not required or cannot be established. The approach enables revisable histories and as-of views but needs trustworthy temporal semantics, correction lineage and evaluation that distinguishes identity truth from attribute-state truth.
